# From £255 to £16 CPL: Transforming Lead Generation for Luxury Architectural Finishes

Joe Short
Feb 13
2 min read



In the world of Luxury Architectural Finishes, lead generation is often the difference between a thriving order book and a quiet showroom. For one of our clients in this specialist sector, the challenge wasn't a lack of ambition—it was a digital marketing strategy that had become prohibitively expensive.

When Visible Ads took over the account in late April 2025, the client was facing a staggering Cost Per Lead (CPL) of £255. For many businesses, these figures are simply unsustainable.




Here is how we turned the tide, slashed costs, and scaled volume simultaneously.



The Turning Point: Immediate Impact



Our first priority was to stabilise the ship. By refining targeting and overhauling the existing campaign structure to better suit high-end architectural products, the results were almost instantaneous. Within our first full month of management, we reduced the CPL from that £255 peak down to £35.22.




However, we didn't stop there. Through continuous optimisation and rigorous data analysis, we have kept the CPL consistently around the £20 mark. In fact, January 2026 has proven to be our strongest month on record, with the CPL dropping further to just £16.38.
