# Serving Up Success: How Visible Ads Transformed an F&amp;B eCommerce Brand’s Google Ads

Joe Short
Feb 18
2 min read

In the fast-paced world of Food and Beverage (F&B) eCommerce, standing out requires more than just a great product; it requires a surgical approach to digital advertising. Recently, Visible Ads took the reins of a UK-based F&B brand that was struggling to find its footing. By implementing structured campaigns and performance-driven best practices, we didn't just improve their numbers—we revolutionised their bottom line.



The Challenge: Wasted Spend and Stagnant Growth

Before partnering with us, the client faced a common industry hurdle: high ad spend with diminishing returns. Their account lacked a cohesive structure, resulting in "keyword cannibalisation" and a failure to capture high-intent shoppers at the right moment. The objective was clear: increase efficiency and scale revenue without simply throwing more money at the problem.



The Visible Ads Strategy: Structure and Precision

Our turnaround plan focused on three core pillars:

Granular Campaign Architecture: We moved away from "catch-all" campaigns, instead segmenting products by margin and performance. This allowed for precise budget allocation toward the highest-value items.

Smart Bidding Optimisation: By refining conversion tracking and feeding the algorithm higher-quality first-party data, we enabled Google’s AI to bid more aggressively for users likely to have a high Average Order Value (AOV).

Creative Resonance: In the F&B niche, "appetite appeal" is everything. We optimised ad copy and visual assets to highlight the brand's unique selling points, ensuring the messaging resonated with the British consumer's preference for authenticity and quality.
