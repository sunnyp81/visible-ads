## CFW's Challenge: Navigating a Changing Landscape

Like many businesses, CFW faced significant challenges, including stagnant post-COVID sales and the loss of a key supermarket client. Recognising the potential of D2C ecommerce, they sought a partner to help them capitalise on this opportunity.

## Visible Ads: A Catalyst for Growth

Visible Ads stepped in to revitalise CFW's online presence and drive sales through strategic paid search campaigns on Google and Amazon.

### Our Strategic Approach:

- **Targeted Audience Engagement:** We identified and engaged high-income households and catering businesses through tailored Google and Amazon Ads campaigns.
- **Performance-Driven Optimisation:** We continuously scaled ad budgets to maximize visibility and sales while maintaining a strict minimum Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) target.
- **Conversion Rate Enhancement:** We optimised product listings, implemented promotions, and introduced "Subscribe & Save" options to encourage repeat purchases.
- **Transparent Data Insights:** We provided CFW with real-time performance data through custom dashboards and reports.

### Overcoming Obstacles:

We addressed CFW's initial lack of D2C expertise through comprehensive training and support. We resolved technical challenges, including data feed errors and tracking implementation, and ensured full compliance with Amazon's HFSS regulations.

### Exceptional Results:

Our collaboration with CFW yielded remarkable results:

- **Google Ads:** Achieved a 1000%+ ROAS, driving substantial revenue growth through strategic scaling.
- **Amazon Ads:** Delivered a 700% ROAS, fuelling significant growth and high rates of subscription renewals.
- **Overall D2C Sales:** Increased UK D2C sales by 68% within 12 months.
- **Organic Traffic Growth:** Achieved a 66% increase in organic traffic through dedicated SEO training.
